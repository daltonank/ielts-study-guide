# CURRICULUM_SPEC.md

## IELTS Academic C1 • UA+EN Curriculum Specification

This document defines the approved learning architecture and quantitative curriculum targets.

---

## 1. Curriculum Goal

Prepare an advanced Ukrainian-speaking learner for IELTS Academic Band 7.0–8.0 by converting strong general English into accurate, strategic, timed exam performance.

The curriculum must train both language and task execution.

---

## 2. Pedagogical Loop

Core learning sequence:

**Understand → Recognize → Guided Practice → Independent Practice → Timed Application → Feedback → Error Review → Retest → Mastery**

The system may vary the surface sequence by skill, but it must preserve evidence-based progression.

Reading established a successful family-module pattern:

**Learn → See → Think → Guided Practice → Independent Practice → Challenge → Timed Round → Review → Error Diagnosis → Mastery Check**

Reuse the pattern where it fits. Do not force it mechanically onto every skill.

---

## 3. Language Model

English is primary.

Ukrainian support is appropriate for:

- difficult concepts;
- contrastive grammar;
- Ukrainian-to-English transfer patterns;
- vocabulary meaning;
- strategy;
- corrective feedback.

Avoid a fully mirrored translation model.

British English conventions are preferred where relevant to IELTS preparation.

- 1,315 Oxford C1 entries
- 570 Academic Word List entries/families
- 1,784 normalized unique Study Bank entries
- 100 Oxford/AWL overlaps
- 100 Starter-set items

Study priorities:

1. AWL sublists 1–3.
2. High-priority IELTS rows.
3. words emerging from skill errors.
4. collocation.
5. paraphrase families.
6. productive use.
7. spaced review.

A vocabulary item is not mastered by recognition alone.

Full vocabulary mastery concept:

1. recognize meaning;
2. recall English from Ukrainian;
3. use/select a natural collocation;
4. use correctly in at least two separate IELTS-style responses.

## 6. G4 Writing Task 1 — COMPLETE

### Quantitative minimum

- 7 visual families
- ≥60 micro-exercises
- ≥20 full Task 1 prompts

### Completed inventory

- 7 / 7 visual families
- 21 original visuals
- 70 micro-exercises
- 21 full timed prompts
- 4 foundation modules, 7 family modules
- 12-category error taxonomy

### Visual families

1. line graphs
2. bar charts
3. pie charts
4. tables
5. process diagrams
6. maps/plans
7. mixed/multiple visuals

### Required curriculum capabilities

Train:

- task interpretation;
- identification of key features;
- overview construction;
- grouping;
- comparison instead of list-like description;
- trend language;
- quantity/proportion language;
- approximation;
- tense selection;
- sentence control;
- lexical variation without data distortion;
- data precision;
- paragraph organization;
- avoiding causal claims not supported by the graphic;
- avoiding personal opinion;
- planning;
- timed drafting;
- self-review;
- common Ukrainian-speaker grammar and article/preposition issues.

### Exercise types

The bank should include a meaningful mixture such as:

- feature selection;
- overview selection;
- comparison building;
- data-to-sentence transformation;
- sentence correction;
- grouping;
- paragraph ordering;
- trend-language choice;
- grammar correction;
- paraphrase without numerical distortion;
- full planning;
- full timed response.

### Visual/data sourcing

Use original visuals/data created for the product or clearly safe official/open sources.

### Feedback

Practice feedback may describe performance relative to IELTS criteria but must be labeled as training guidance, not official scoring.

## 8. G6 Grammar Clinic

### Minimum

- ≥20 modules
- ≥250 items

### Approach

Grammar instruction must be contrastive, practical, and IELTS-connected.

Prioritize patterns that affect advanced Ukrainian-speaking learners, including as appropriate:

- articles;
- countability;
- prepositions;
- tense/aspect;
- conditionals;
- complex clauses;
- agreement;
- word order;
- punctuation;
- relative clauses;
- nominalization;
- modality;
- comparison;
- cohesion;
- sentence boundaries;
- participle structures;
- precision in academic statements.

Grammar errors should feed the shared Error Log and review model.

## 9. G6 Paraphrasing Academy

### Minimum

- ≥100 exercises

- active/passive reformulation;
- nominalization and den nominalization;

## 10. G6 Pronunciation

Pronunciation must support Speaking intelligibility rather than accent erasure.

Train as appropriate:

- stress;
- rhythm;
- chunking;
- connected speech;
- difficult consonant/vowel contrasts;
- endings;
- word stress;
- sentence stress;
- intelligibility;
- self-monitoring.

Use Ukrainian-specific transfer notes where useful.

## 13. G9 Error, Review, Adaptation and Mocking

### Error taxonomy

Each academy should classify errors in a skill-relevant way.

Reading precedent includes:
- evidence-location failure;
- paraphrase failure;
- inference error;
- context-vocabulary error;
- distractor selection;
- timing;
- false/not-given confusion;
- main-idea/heading confusion;
- answer-format/carelessness.

Writing, Speaking, Listening, Grammar, and Vocabulary should define their own useful categories.

### Review

Review scheduling should reflect:
- recency;
- repeated errors;
- mastery weakness;
- due vocabulary;
- failed practice;
- later mock outcomes.

### Adaptive recommendations

Recommendations should be explainable and testable.

### Mock Center

Mocking should progress toward realistic IELTS conditions. Full realism belongs here, not in short academy drills.

---

## 14. Content Construction Rules

Every bank must be checked for:

- originality/legal source;
- appropriate level;
- natural English;
- answer validity;
- plausible distractors where applicable;
- unique identifiers;
- required metadata;
- explanation quality;
- consistent scoring;
- Ukrainian quality where used.

Avoid artificial complexity and rare-word stuffing.

---

## 15. Curriculum Completion Rule

A curriculum requirement is complete only when:

1. required content exists;
2. it is integrated into the learner flow;
3. it is usable on target viewports;
4. state/progress behavior works;
5. content QA passes;
6. automated/functional validation passes where specified;
7. regression against earlier phases passes;
8. the gate report records evidence.
