# Reading Content QA Note

Phase 3 content was checked using both automated inventory tests and manual cross-family review.

## Manual review sample
At least one text/question set was inspected from every question family, with additional attention to mastery/timed examples. Checks included:
- the correct answer is supported by the passage;
- False/No answers genuinely contradict rather than merely fail to appear;
- Not Given answers are not inferable from hidden assumptions;
- headings describe the whole paragraph rather than a single example;
- matching-feature attribution is unambiguous;
- completion answers occur in the passage and fit the gap;
- inference answers are the weakest defensible conclusion rather than an overstatement;
- language is suitable for advanced exam preparation and free of commercial copyrighted test text.

## Curriculum scope
The 60 texts are focused practice passages/extracts. They teach question mechanics and evidence habits. They are not presented as full IELTS three-section mock papers. Full-length integration is reserved for the Mock Center phase so the product preserves the distinction between skill isolation and exam simulation.
