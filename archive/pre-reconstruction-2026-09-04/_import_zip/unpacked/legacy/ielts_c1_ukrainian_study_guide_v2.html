Mini drill: True / False / Not Given

Text: The university introduced optional evening seminars in 2025. Attendance was initially low, but participation doubled during the second semester.

Statement: Evening seminars became compulsory in the second semester.

False. The passage explicitly says the seminars were optional. Higher participation does not make them compulsory.
Writing lab • Task 1

Visual analysis before sentence production

Find the pattern first. Numbers are evidence, not the structure of the answer.

Four-step routine

  1. Identify: graph type, units, time period.
  2. Select: highest, lowest, major rise/fall, stability, anomaly.
  3. Group: put related trends together.
  4. Write: introduction → overview → detail 1 → detail 2.
Rule: the overview should communicate the dominant patterns without drowning in individual figures.

Ukrainian → English grammar clinic

Train recurring transfer errors in context

Articles

I bought ___ car yesterday. ___ car is already having problems.

First mention: a car. Known reference after introduction: the car.

Prepositions

depend on
interested in
responsible for

Countability

information, advice, research, evidence

Practise these as uncountable nouns unless a different specific construction is required.

Correction drill

Correct: She gave me an useful advice about the exam.

She gave me useful advice about the exam. “Advice” is uncountable here, so it does not take “an”.
